01 ABOUT THE ARTIST

DJ YUVRAJ / PROFESSIONAL DJ & ARTIST

BUILT FOR THE ROOM.
REMEMBERED AFTER.

DJ YUVRAJ brings a polished, high-energy sound to weddings, clubs, festivals, private celebrations, corporate events and destination experiences. Every set is shaped around the crowd, the venue and the moment.

DJ YUVRAJ is a dynamic Indian DJ, music producer, and remix artist known for his energetic performances, powerful remixes, and distinctive approach to commercial and Bollywood music. With a strong passion for music and an instinct for reading the crowd, DJ Yuvraj brings together the latest Bollywood hits, Punjabi beats, commercial anthems, and high-energy remixes to create an unforgettable experience for every audience. Over the years, DJ Yuvraj has developed his own musical identity through creative remixing, seamless DJ sets, and a deep understanding of what keeps the dance floor moving. His music reflects a blend of contemporary sounds, desi energy, and modern electronic production, making his sets suitable for clubs, weddings, private celebrations, festivals, and large-scale events. Beyond live performances, DJ Yuvraj is actively involved in music production and official remix projects, continuously working on new sounds and collaborations while building his presence in the Indian music and entertainment industry. His vision is simple — to turn every beat into an experience. DJ Yuvraj — Feel the Beat. Live the Moment.
