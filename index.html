DJ YUVRAJ brings a polished, high-energy sound to weddings, clubs, festivals, private celebrations, corporate events and destination experiences. Every set is shaped around the crowd, the venue and the moment.

DJ YUVRAJ is a dynamic Indian DJ, music producer, and remix artist known for energetic performances, powerful remixes, and a distinctive approach to commercial and Bollywood music.

With a strong instinct for reading the crowd, DJ Yuvraj blends Bollywood hits, Punjabi beats, commercial anthems and high-energy remixes to create unforgettable experiences.

Beyond live performances, DJ Yuvraj is actively involved in music production and official remix projects, continuously building his musical identity and presence in the Indian entertainment industry.

His vision is simple — to turn every beat into an experience.
